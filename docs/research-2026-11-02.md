# Site research, 2 Nov 2026

Six read-only audits of www.blokblokstudio.com (performance, conversion,
accessibility, local SEO and German market, competitors and content, legal
and security). This file is the merged, prioritised result. Items marked
DONE shipped on 2 Nov. Items marked DECIDE need Chase before anyone builds.

## Shipped 2 Nov (performance)

- Globe (react-globe.gl + three, about 2 MB raw) now loads only when its
  section is within 400px of the viewport; three is no longer in the
  initial bundle; geojson fetched on the same trigger; hex resolution 2 on
  phones; no auto-rotate under prefers-reduced-motion.
- Result, desktop Chrome, live homepage before scrolling: JavaScript
  3,553 KB -> 123 KB transferred, long tasks 178 (19.3 s) -> 2 (0.15 s),
  FCP 496 ms, LCP 632 ms. Globe still appears about 4 s after scrolling
  to it and its hex build costs about 5 s of main thread once visible.
- Client logos resized (bronco 152 -> 9 KB, real texas 62 -> 14 KB,
  exotic ripz 60 -> 15 KB, kds png -> webp); first Work card no longer
  preloaded; /images cached one week, /data one year.

Legal and security (developer side)
24. Google Analytics loads under the marketing toggle while /cookies and
    /privacy say the site runs no analytics. Either delete the GA default
    (the hard-coded default in AdsPixels.tsx) or add an Analytics category, list
    `_ga` in /cookies and describe GA in /privacy. Needed before item 22.
25. Google Calendar iframe on /thanks loads before consent
    (`loading="eager"`). Two-click placeholder: "Load calendar".
26. EU ODR platform sentence (impressum.odr_1/odr_2, terms.dispute_eu):
    the platform closed 20 July 2025. Remove; keep the VSBG sentence.
27. GDPR delete is a GET link in an email; link scanners can prefetch it
    and erase a lead. Make it a confirmation page with a POST button.
28. Internal admin routes compare the shared secret with `!==` and accept
    `?key=` in the query string. Header only, `timingSafeEqual`.
29. Honour Global Privacy Control in `mayTrack()` and say so in /privacy.
30. Consent record: store user agent, page URL and privacy-policy version
    on the Lead row; version the start.consent_* sentence like the
    marketing one.
31. Privacy policy Art. 13 gaps: controller legal name and postal address,
    GA4 and the database host as recipients, name the EU-US Data Privacy
    Framework for Vercel, Resend, Cloudflare, Google and Meta, concrete
    retention periods, legal basis per purpose, a standalone right-to-
    object paragraph, the Berlin supervisory authority, and a new "last
    updated" date.
32. Security headers: HSTS has `preload` but the domain is not submitted
    (submit at hstspreload.org or drop the token); CSP still allows
    `unsafe-inline` and `unsafe-eval` (nonces via proxy.ts); add a
    Report-Only endpoint before tightening; X-XSS-Protection can go.
33. Rate limiting is an in-memory Map per instance. Add a Vercel WAF
    rate-limit rule on /api/contact, /api/call, /api/newsletter,
    /api/gdpr/request. Drop the "SOC 2 compliant" code comments.

## Tier 2: DECIDE (Chase)

A. Impressum. A registered-agent mailbox is not a ladungsfähige
   Anschrift. With a Berlin establishment, German-language pages and
   German clients, § 5 DDG and § 18 MStV (blog) call for the Berlin
   business address, the entity register number, the managing member, and a
   USt-IdNr if one exists. Lawyer questions: Zweigniederlassung
   registration (§ 13d HGB), permanent establishment for tax, German VAT
   ID.
B. Art. 27 representative or EU establishment statement in /privacy
   (lawyer call). Choice of law and forum in /terms ("applicable laws" is
   empty; a forum clause against consumers is invalid).
C. Phone number: DECIDED 2 Nov, no phone number on the site. Contact
   stays form, quiz and calendar. Do not propose again.
D. Written guarantee. Low risk: "no change orders during build, no
   surprise fees between quote and launch". Higher risk: deposit refunded
   if the first design is rejected. Every benchmark competitor has one.
E. Reviews and proof: Chase's answer 2 Nov is that the video
   testimonials (Luki, Kofi) are the proof. Build task: embed each video
   on its matching case study, on the trainer page and on /pricing, with
   VideoObject schema (exists on Ideas). Google Business Profile and
   directories remain optional; if ever added, no AggregateRating schema
   on #organization (self-serving, ineligible) and an Echtheit note next
   to any written reviews (§ 5b UWG).
F. Releases for the Luki and Kofi videos (image, voice, name, scope,
   withdrawal) and client sign-off on every published metric. If any
   discount was given for a testimonial, disclose it next to the video
   (FTC 16 CFR 465). Caption option: "Unpaid testimonial."
G. Testimonial quotes on the site that are not in the transcripts (Kofi,
   Exotic Ripz, Luki on the trainer page): confirm or remove.
H. Pricing framing: deposit split (50/50 or three payments) and whether
   care is "first three months included" or "from $550/month after
   launch". Needs the real terms.
I. A low-commitment alternative to the call (10-minute video teardown or
   free audit) for "just exploring" leads. Capacity decision.
J. Missing proof: metrics for Coach Luki, KDS, The New School and Grant
   Parkhurst; logos for the remaining clients (PAL, New School, Exotic
   Ripz permission); per-page OG images; Bridget's phone for the vCard;
   a 300-word founder story for /about.
K. Hero copy. The conversion audit wants the outcome and the numbers in
   the hero ("Five pages from $6,500, live in 4 to 6 weeks") instead of
   "Building brands, blok by blok. / Web Design Studio in Berlin". This is

L. Globe dot density: resolution 2 everywhere would cut the remaining 5 s

## Competitive picture (for context)

Berlin agencies ranking for "Webdesign Berlin" all show: German-only
pages, EUR "ab" prices, a 030 landline, a Google review count with a
verification note, named local Referenzen, a blog posting one to five
times a month. None shows case-study numbers; none is hand-coded. US
small-business studios add: timeline in the hero, written guarantees,
12 to 19 FAQs before the final CTA, founder face, phone in the header,
monthly or installment framing. What only we have: numbers on SMB case
studies, hosting and Core Web Vitals in the project price, full code
ownership, a $3B client, a five-question diagnostic quiz.

Full reports with sources were produced by the six research agents on
2 Nov 2026 and summarised here; the measured numbers come from Lighthouse
12 (mobile preset) and Chrome PerformanceObserver on the live site.
