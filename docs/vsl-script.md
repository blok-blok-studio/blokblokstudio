# Blok Blok Studio — VSL Script (2026)

**Format:** 3:30–4:00 min · real face on camera (not AI avatar — trust data favors humans),
burned-in captions (67%+ watch muted on mobile), pattern interrupt every 20–30s,
ONE call to action. Shot list assumes iPhone + good light is enough; b-roll from
existing client sites and the tracker dashboard.

**Where it runs:** `/vsl` page (video + one button), retargeting ads (cut-down 60s
version), and the follow-up email to leads who didn't book.

## PRODUCTION NOTES (2026 standards)

- **Length:** 3:30–4:00 for the /vsl page. Cut a 60s version (Hook → 1 proof → CTA)
  for retargeting ads and a 30s vertical for social.
- **Captions:** burned-in, high-contrast. Mandatory — most mobile viewers are muted.
- **First frame:** your face mid-gesture + hook text overlay (thumbnails/autoplay
  first frames decide watch-through).
- **Pattern interrupts:** change the visual every 20–30s (b-roll, zoom punch-in,
  text card). Retention collapses on static talking-head.
- **One CTA:** the page shows exactly one button. No nav, no links up top.
- **Proof rule:** every claim on screen must be traceable to a real client
  (Luke Satterly, Coach Kofi) — no invented numbers, ever (ad policy + legal).
- **Record:** 4K horizontal master → crop 1:1 and 9:16 cuts. Clean audio matters
  more than clean video: lav mic or quiet room.
- **Measure:** watch-time drop-off (aim >50% at 1:00), button CTR from the page,
  booked calls. Swap hooks first if drop-off is early; swap offer framing if
  people watch but don't click.
